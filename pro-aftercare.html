02 · Why artists will love it

Made for the way you work

Here is what the balm is being developed to do.

  • One balm, four jobs. Prep, tattoo, aftercare and lamination from a single product, so there is less on your trolley and less to reorder.
  • Made for lips and brows. Developed for delicate lip skin and brow hair, not adapted from a body tattoo balm.
  • Nourishing at every stage. Keeps lips nourished and comfortable before, during and after their tattoo.
  • The same balm goes home with your client. What you use in the studio is what they use afterwards, so their care stays consistent.
  • An exclusive formula. Developed for Crystal Cosmetics and not sold under any other label.
  • Tried and tested first. Used in Crystal's own treatments before it reaches yours.
03 · Founding artists

  • Founding wholesale pricing, shared with you before you decide anything.
  • A say in the sizes: single-client jars to hand over at the appointment, studio packs, or both.
