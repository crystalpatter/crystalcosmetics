Brow Lamination · Varsity Lakes

Your own brows, brushed up

Brow lamination sets your natural brow hairs in a brushed-up, fuller-looking shape. Crystal finishes with a tint and a precision wax and shape, so your brows look groomed and defined without daily gel or pencil.

There's no tattooing involved, so it's a great way to try a fuller brow look. If you'd like colour that stays for years, see powder brows.

Every appointment is with Crystal herself, at her studio at Shop 4, 195 Varsity Parade, Varsity Lakes — an easy trip from Robina, Burleigh, Mudgeeraba, Reedy Creek and Mermaid Waters.
