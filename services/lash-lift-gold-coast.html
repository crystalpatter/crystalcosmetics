Your own lashes lifted, curled and tinted for definition — in Crystal's Varsity Lakes studio.

Lash Lift · Varsity Lakes

Wake up with lifted lashes

A lash lift gives your natural lashes a lifted curl, and the tint adds definition at the roots and tips, so your eyes look awake without a daily coat of mascara.

It works with your own lashes and follows their natural growth cycle, so the result lasts around 6–8 weeks. Pair it with brow lamination for a full brow and lash refresh.

Every appointment is with Crystal herself, at her studio at Shop 4, 195 Varsity Parade, Varsity Lakes — an easy trip from Robina, Burleigh, Mudgeeraba, Reedy Creek and Mermaid Waters.
