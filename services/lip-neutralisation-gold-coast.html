Lip Neutralisation · Varsity Lakes

An even base for your lips

Lip neutralisation is a cosmetic tattoo treatment for lips that are naturally darker or uneven in tone. Pigment is chosen to work with your own colouring, so your lips sit on a more even base.

Every set of lips starts from a different place, so how many sessions you need depends on your starting tone. Crystal will talk you through a plan for your lips at your consultation.

Every appointment is with Crystal herself, at her studio at Shop 4, 195 Varsity Parade, Varsity Lakes — an easy trip from Robina, Burleigh, Mudgeeraba, Reedy Creek and Mermaid Waters.

Lip Neutralisation FAQs

A cosmetic tattoo treatment for naturally darker or uneven lip tone. It works with your own colouring to give your lips a more even base tone.

$550 for the initial treatment, and $200 for a touch-up at 4–6 weeks or a colour refresh later on.

It depends on your starting lip tone. Some lips need more than one session to even out, so Crystal will give you an honest plan at your consultation.

A topical anaesthetic is used throughout. Most clients describe a light scratching sensation rather than pain.

Lips are generally healed within 7–10 days. Colour looks stronger at first and settles over the following weeks.

Eat beforehand, skip alcohol for 24 hours, and hold off ibuprofen, aspirin and fish oil for 72 hours, as they can thin the blood. Come in sun-safe, and avoid recent waxing or tinting near the treatment area.
