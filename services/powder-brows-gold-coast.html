Powder & Ombré Brows · Varsity Lakes

Brows that are already done

Powder brows, also called ombré brows, use fine shading: tiny dots of pigment built up gradually for a soft, filled-in look, like brow powder that doesn't wipe off. They're shaded rather than drawn as individual hairs, so the finish is soft and even.

They're built to hold their shape through workouts and long days, so you can stop redrawing the same brows every morning.

Every appointment is with Crystal herself, at her studio at Shop 4, 195 Varsity Parade, Varsity Lakes — an easy trip from Robina, Burleigh, Mudgeeraba, Reedy Creek and Mermaid Waters.
